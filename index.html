R5 | LEADERSHIP

DIPLOMACY · ULTIMATE MATTER SOLVER
  • Represent and protect MOR's interests within the Kingdom and with other Alliances.
  • Handle high-level diplomacy.
  • Support the R4 team when needed.
  • When an important issue cannot be resolved by the R4 team, the R5 makes the final call.
“Final authority should be the last step, not the first.”

Day-to-day Alliance leadership belongs to the R4 team.

🛡

R4 | ALLIANCE COMMAND

PLAN · ORGANISE · EXECUTE
  • Run the Alliance and turn strategy into action.
  • Lead events, war preparation and execution.
  • Manage territory, recruitment and development.
  • Communicate with members.
  • Gather and share information.
  • Solve problems and support members.
  • Keep the Alliance organised and active.
  • Support the R5 on diplomacy when required.
  • Own specific areas (Events | War | Territory | Recruitment | Members | Communications).
R4 IS A JOB, NOT A REWARD.

One team. Clear roles. Real impact.

👥

ALLIANCE MEMBERS

RESPONSIBILITIES & RIGHTS
YOU SHOULD:
  • Be active and read Alliance messages.
  • Participate in events when possible.
  • Follow agreed strategy.
  • Respect Alliance agreements and rules.
  • Help teammates.
  • Communicate absences to an R4 in advance.
  • Avoid actions that put the Alliance at risk.
⚠ ACTIVITY & ATTENDANCEIf you cannot attend Alliance activities, inform an R4 beforehand whenever possible. Unexplained inactivity or repeated failure to participate will result in progressive demotion. Three consecutive weeks without participation or communication may result in removal from the Alliance.
COMMUNICATION PROTECTS YOUR PLACE.
YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO:
  • Be treated with respect.
  • Ask, suggest and disagree respectfully.
  • Receive important information.
  • Raise concerns with an R4 or R5.
  • Put real life before the game.

EVENTS & WAR

PREPARE · COORDINATE · EXECUTE · IMPROVE
  • Clear objective, time and R4 commander.
  • Strategy and player instructions.
  • Follow current Alliance instructions (e.g. Bear Hunt rallies, heroes, formations).
  • For Castle, KvK and other major operations, follow the plan and your commander.
  • During the battle: execute the plan.
  • After the battle: question and improve the plan.
AFTER EVERY EVENT WE ASK:
  • What worked?
  • What didn't?
  • What did we learn?
  • What changes next time?

It's not about who screwed up. It's about how we get better.

⚖️

DISAGREEMENTS & PROBLEMS

SOLVE IT TOGETHER.
  1. Talk directly if appropriate.
  2. Escalate to an R4.
  3. Escalate to the R5 if needed.
  4. R5 makes the final call.
  • Challenge the decision, not the person.
  • Keep personal issues private.
  • R4s disagreements should be resolved within leadership before communicating one position to the Alliance.
  • Correct privately. Praise publicly.
  • No retaliation for raising concerns in good faith.
  • Disagreement is normal. Drama is optional.
